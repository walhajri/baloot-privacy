Privacy policy — Baloot

Last updated 22 September 2026

Baloot is a scorekeeping app for the card game Baloot. It collects no personal data of any kind.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

Your match scores, round history and settings are saved on your own device so the app can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is not readable by anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Network access

The app contains no networking code. It works fully offline and makes no requests to any server, including ours.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected. The app contains no third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
